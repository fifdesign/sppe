SPPE v1.0Seamless Pixel Pattern Editor

A pixel-art editor for seamless textures and patterns, made for tablets with a stylus and for desktop. With Wrap on, the canvas repeats: whatever leaves one edge comes back on the opposite edge, so the tile always fits together. Everything stays in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere.

Top bar

New
A new canvas of any width and height up to 4096 px per side and 4.2 million pixels in total. Strip presets (for example 1024 × 4096) set the matching wrap mode.
Open
Opens an .sppe project (older .pxwrap and .json projects work too) or an image. In the import dialog you set the canvas size and fit, and can snap the colors to any palette.
Save
Saves the project as an .sppe file with all layers and their settings, the palette, the wrap mode and your own stamps.
Undo / Redo
The arrows, or two- and three-finger taps.
Wrap
Both directions, only horizontal (X), only vertical (Y) or off. One axis suits side-scroller strips and tall walls with real edges. Every tool follows the active axes.
Grid
Pixel grid, plus the tile borders around the canvas.
Pencil only

Tools

Each tool shows its options in a bar above the canvas. Brush shapes take little room there: the bar shows the current shape and the last ones you used; tap the current shape (▾) to open all 284 shapes in their groups, and pick one to close it again. Tap the active tool again to hide that bar, tap once more to bring it back. A dashed outline shows that the options are hidden. The size slider works for every brush: hold it and drag for 1 px steps, or use − and + or type a number.

B Pencil
Draws pixels in the main color. Square or round tip, or any of 284 brush shapes in 14 groups (Shapes, Geometry, Forms, Symbols, Food, Items, Letters, Numbers, Nature, Effects and more), filled or as outline with a line width. Pixel perfect removes doubled corner pixels in 1 px strokes. Stabilizer (lazy rope) smooths lines; set the rope length.
N Line mode
For Pencil, Eraser and Dither. Drag a line, or tap point after point. Connect points starts each line where the last ended, New line (or Esc) starts fresh, Snap angles (or Shift) keeps 0°, 45°, 90° and 2:1. Styles: Straight, Wave, Zigzag, Arcs, Scallops, Saw teeth and Blocks, each with Period and Height in pixels. Shadow adds a second line in the 2nd color 1 to 5 px below. Full width / Full height: one tap runs the line across the whole canvas with the period fitted so it wraps seamlessly.
D Dither
Paints a dither pattern from 12% to 75%. Gaps: 2nd color fills the gaps with the 2nd color.
A Spray
Airbrush dots in the main color. Uses the same 284 brush shapes as the Pencil, filled or as outline: the dots land only inside the shape (Round is the classic airbrush). Flow sets how fast the dots come (stylus pressure adds flow). Intensity sets how much of the shape can fill up in one stroke: 20% stays a light sprinkle however long you hold, 100% fills it completely. Spacing keeps the dots at least that many pixels apart. Holding still keeps spraying, it does not pick a color.
S Shade
Darker or Lighter, one step per stroke, with the same 284 brush shapes as the Pencil, filled or as outline. Snap to palette uses the next darker or lighter color of the active palette. Reverse takes shading back step by step.
U Smear
Pushes pixels along the stroke with adjustable strength, in any of the Pencil's brush shapes, filled or as outline. Snap to palette keeps every pixel a palette color.
E Eraser
Erases with the same tips and shapes as the Pencil, including Line mode.
F Fill
Flood fill. In Wrap mode it continues across the edges, and on repeating layers it fills every copy.
I Pick
Picks a color from the canvas. You can also hold the pen or finger still on the canvas while painting.
R Select
Rectangle, square, ellipse, circle, lasso and magic wand (tolerance, contiguous, all layers). Select all, Invert, Grow 1 px, Shrink 1 px, Clear selection. Tools only paint inside the selection. The selection bar offers Delete pixels, Fill, Copy to new layer and Make stamp.
O Rect
Rectangle or ellipse, filled or as outline with a line width. Drag for any size (the size shows at the bottom); Square / circle or Shift keeps it even; a tap places the exact W × H from the options. Works across the wrap edges and on repeating layers.
K Stamp
Stamps any brush shape, filled or as outline, or one of your own stamps. Rotate 90°, Flip ↔ and ↕. Grid snaps the stamp's top-left corner to 8, 16, 32 or 64 px, ideal for placing modules. Tap to stamp, drag to place.
C Clone
Copies pixels by painting, like the clone stamp in Photoshop. Point: tap Set point (or Alt-click) on the spot to copy from, then paint somewhere else; a ring shows where the copy comes from. Region: select an area with Select, then tap Use selection; painting anywhere repeats that region like a tile, so a stroke lays down the region again and again. Aligned keeps the same offset for every stroke (off: each stroke starts from the source again). From picks where the copy comes from (the active layer, All visible, or any single layer). To is the layer it is painted on; choose another layer or + New layer to keep the clones separate from the original. Uses the Pencil's brush shapes and size; empty pixels of the source are skipped.
Own stamps
Select an area and tap Make stamp. Take the pixels from this layer, all visible layers or the layers you tick, and give it a name. The colored stamp appears under Own shapes in the Stamp tool. Size 1:1, 2×, 3×, 4× or the slider; pixels stay crisp. Own stamps are kept in this browser and saved in your projects. Delete stamp needs two taps.
T Transform
Move, scale, flip and rotate the layer or the selection. Keep ratio, Snap 50%, quick 50% / 100% / 200%, Rotate 90° or any angle with snap steps. Pick an anchor, use the opposite side, or drag your own pivot. Apply (Enter) or Cancel (Esc).
M Offset
Drag to slide the layer around the tile and check the seams. ½ Offset all layers moves everything by half a tile. With Wrap X or Y it moves only along that axis.
Edit map
Appears when you edit a pattern layer (see Layers). Pick a module, tap a cell on the canvas to place it, tap it again to turn it 90°, hold it to mirror it ↔. Turn and Flip set how new cells are placed. Done returns to your last tool.

Colors

Color modes
Four pickers, switched with the icons under COLOR: Disc (hue ring outside, saturation and brightness in the disc), Classic (square and hue bar), Harmony (a wheel with matching colors: Complementary, Split complementary, Analogous, Triadic or Tetradic; drag the big dot, tap a swatch below to use that color, Brightness slider underneath) and Value (sliders and numbers for hue, saturation, brightness, red, green and blue).
Main and 2nd color
The picker and the hex input set the main color. Tap the small square or press X to swap.
Palettes
Classic palettes, the SPPE 32 set and 101 theme palettes: Beach, Winter, Forest, Desert, Ocean, Sunset & Sky, Autumn, Spring, Night & City, Fantasy, Retro & Games, Food & Candy.
Your palettes
My palette and any number of your own named palettes: + Add color, Remove, + New palette, rename in the name field, Delete.
From image
Builds a palette from the canvas or any image. Choose 2 to 64 colors, check the preview, then save it as a new named palette or replace / add to the selected one.
Shades
Hold any color swatch (palette, Recent, Harmony) for a moment: a small window shows 16 shades of it from very bright to dark. Tap one to use it; the ring marks the shade closest to the original.
Recent
The colors you painted with last, one tap away. Hide folds the color panel to make room.
In projects
Saving a project also stores the current palette with its name and colors.

Layers

Basics
Up to 16 layers. Drag a layer by its grip (⠿) to move it up or down; with a mouse you can drag the whole row. + new layer, Duplicate, Import image, Delete. The eye shows or hides a layer, the lock keeps alpha (paint only on existing pixels). Name, Blend mode and Opacity per layer. Move up, Move down, Merge down, Clear (erases every pixel of the layer, Undo brings them back), Split by color… (ticked colors move from the layer onto new layers of their own; handy when you painted two things on one layer by accident), Transform.
Hue, saturation, contrast
Under Opacity. Changes the colors of the layer without touching the pixels, so you can always go back: Reset color, or double-tap a slider to set it to 0. Apply to pixels bakes the colors in. Export, merge and stamps use the adjusted colors.
Repeat
Tap a size (8, 16, 32 … px) or Custom… for any size across and down. Bake stops the repeat and keeps all copies as normal pixels, so you can then paint each copy on its own. The top-left area of that size repeats over the whole layer, and whatever you paint shows up in every copy. Turning it on copies the top-left area over the rest of the layer; Undo brings the old pixels back. Use it for a tile running down a long strip, teeth every 16 px or waves every 32 px.
Pattern layer
A tile in a tile. "+ Pattern layer…" builds a layer live from module layers. Set the cell size, columns and rows, and pick which layer is module 1, 2, 3 …. "+ Module" takes the next free layer, or makes a new empty "Module" layer. Module k is the top-left cell of its layer. Every cell keeps the full resolution of its module: 2 × 2 cells of 16 px make a 32 × 32 tile, and the canvas grows to fit while the other layers repeat to fill it. In the map grid pick a module, tap a cell to place it, tap again to turn it 90° (↻), hold to mirror it (↔). "Edit as text" takes a map like 1 2@90 / 2@270 1h (@90, @180, @270 turn clockwise, h flips ↔, v flips ↕, a dot is empty). Paint on a module and every cell that uses it updates at once. A pattern can be a module of a bigger pattern. Edit pattern…, Edit map (on the canvas) and Flatten (plain pixels) sit in the layer settings.
Edge strip…
In Settings (⚙) and Layers. Turns the square tile into a long strip (2× to 64×, vertical or horizontal). The tile layers keep repeating, Wrap switches to the open axis, and a new Edge layer on top is ready for the border. Undo brings the tile back.

Files

Save / Open
Projects are .sppe files. Open also takes images.
Export PNG
The merged image and/or every layer as its own PNG, 1× to 16×, optionally as a tiled preview sheet (3 × 3, or 3 × 1 / 1 × 3 with one wrap axis). Include module layers decides whether the module layers of patterns go out too. Several files come as one ZIP. Exports are lossless.
Autosave
Your work is kept in this browser (big canvases in the browser database). Save a project to keep it safe.
Install
Open the app in the browser and choose Add to Home Screen. It then works offline too.
